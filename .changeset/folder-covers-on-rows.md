---
'prism': patch
---

Folder covers and the sidebar's arrows now show as soon as a folder appears, from what Prism already knows of your library, instead of each folder asking for them in turn, which on a large library or a network share could take a while. Covers still change as you add, remove, rename or replace photos. A folder with no photos of its own now shows those of the first folder inside it, by name, that has any, however deep they are, where before it showed those of the shallowest folder with photos, so a few folders of folders may show a different cover than before.
