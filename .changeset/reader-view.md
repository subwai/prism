A third view, the reader, shows a folder's photos one below the other, in full, for reading comics and scanned books. It scrolls as a web page does, with the trackpad, Up and Down, Space and Page Up and Down, while Left and Right turn to the next or previous page. It shows only the photos in the folder itself, not its videos or subfolders, and the status bar names the page at the top. A slider in the status bar sets how wide pages are. Like the viewer, it has controls along the top for turning pages, a slideshow that turns a page at a time, and full screen, which double-clicking a page or Cmd-Shift-F also enters. Cmd-G (Ctrl-G on Windows and Linux) and View > Switch View, which replaces Switch Grid/Filmstrip, now step through all three views.
