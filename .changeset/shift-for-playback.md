Keys without Shift now get around and keys with Shift work the photo or video shown: Space opens and closes the preview, Left and Right always move between photos and videos, and Shift-Space plays and pauses, Shift-Left and Shift-Right skip, and Shift-M mutes. If you rebound any of these in your keybindings.json, check they still do what you want.

The preview now belongs to the grid alone: the filmstrip and the reader already show photos large, so Space, Enter and double-clicking there no longer open one over them.
