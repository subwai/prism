While photos' details are read in the background, Prism re-sorts large views far less often. A view sorted by name, size or a file date, without a search or a filter by camera, lens, tag or rating, no longer re-sorts as details come in, and the others re-sort every few seconds rather than every second. For a library of 1.8 million photos, that takes the views' sorting from about a third of a processor core to about a tenth by date taken, and a few percent by name.
