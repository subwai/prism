The activity panel's Folders row now says how up to date your folders are once Prism isn't reading them, such as "Up to date · checked 12 min ago" with a full bar, instead of "Idle" with an empty one. It says so when a full read was paused part-way, when the share is offline, and when the folders haven't been checked yet, and the panel only says the catalog is up to date once every folder has been checked.
