A new name now shows the moment you rename something, in the grid, filmstrip, reader and sidebar, instead of after the file on a network share has been renamed. Stars you choose already showed at once; now they show the same in every place, and pressing several rating keys in a row always ends on the last one. If a rating or rename fails, the old one shows again, and the rename dialog comes back with what you typed and says why.
