Rate photos, videos and folders from one to five stars: press 1 to 5, or click the stars in the status bar, the viewer, a photo's preview or a folder's preview, and choose the same rating again, or press 0, to take it away. The search field filters by rating, keeping everything with at least that many stars. Photos keep their rating in the file, where Lightroom, Bridge and Windows Explorer read it too.
