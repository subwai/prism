'prism': patch
---

What's on screen now comes first whenever Prism reads your disk or network share, ahead of everything it reads in the background, and thumbnails, folder covers and photo details no longer compete with each other for it. Thumbnails you scroll past before their turn are no longer made. In a folder of fewer than 1,000 photos and videos, or a filmstrip of fewer than 1,000, Prism now makes the thumbnails you haven't scrolled to yet in the background, nearest first, so they're ready when you get there.
