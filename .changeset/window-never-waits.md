---
'prism': patch
---

Prism now opens straight to what it knows of your library, however slow your network share is, and even when it's offline. It no longer waits for the share to answer before showing the folder you had open, a folder you open, or a folder's preview: they show what Prism already knows at once and fill in as the share is read. On every launch, Prism now checks every folder of your library in the background, so changes made while it was closed, including deleted folders, show up without you opening each folder.
