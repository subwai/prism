Renaming a folder in Prism is instant however much it holds: its photos keep their thumbnails, details, covers and ratings, and nothing inside it is read from the disk again. Before, a folder of hundreds of thousands of photos on a network share took minutes to read again under its new name. Photos moved out of the way of a move to the Trash, and put back by Undo, go the same way.
